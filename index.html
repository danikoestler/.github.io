About your guide

About your guide

Hi, I’m Daniel, a 22-year-old physics student and Technical Student at CERN.

Alongside my work, I volunteer as a CERN guide because I love sharing my workplace with visitors from around the world.

My CERN tours

I give tours of the Antimatter Factory, also known as AD, the CERN Control Centre, the Data Centre and the ATLAS Visitor Centre.

My goal is to explain CERN’s work in an interesting, approachable and enjoyable way, whether you already know a lot about physics or are discovering it for the first time.

Places you may visit

Depending on the tour, visitors may discover some of CERN’s most important scientific and technical sites.
